## Prohibited content

Do not attempt to bypass provider safety controls or misuse the service to generate abusive content.

The viral dancing Rumpelstiltskin clip that inspired this product is AI-generated. Do not label AI results as authentic footage from a 1987 film. This service is not affiliated with a film studio or the creators of the viral clip. Our AI provider may apply safety checks and reject requests. Automated checks cannot guarantee that every result is suitable. Review outputs before sharing them and identify them as AI-generated. We may refuse requests, remove access to content or suspend accounts for violations, subject to applicable law.

Email [support@rumpelstiltskinai.org](mailto:support@rumpelstiltskinai.org) with the subject “Abuse report”, the relevant link or task identifier, and a description of the concern. Signed-in users may also submit a support ticket. We review reports and take appropriate action; no fixed response-time guarantee is offered in this first version. We cooperate with authorities where legally required. Contact the same address to appeal an account action.
